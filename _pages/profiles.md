## The lab

The Genoplastic Surgery Laboratory is a young lab at the University of Michigan. We bring together **surgeons, molecular biologists, and trainees** with a shared interest in regeneration biology and translational gene therapy.

### Postdoctoral Researchers
_We're actively recruiting._ See [Join us](/join/) — strong backgrounds in molecular biology, mouse genetics, developmental biology, or AAV / gene therapy especially welcome.

### Graduate Students
_Open to motivated students through Michigan's graduate programs._

### Medical Trainees & Students
We host medical students, residents, and fellows interested in research time. Trainees at every career stage are welcome — see [Join us](/join/).
