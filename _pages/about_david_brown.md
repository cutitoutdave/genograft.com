# David A. Brown, MD, PhD

**Principal Investigator** · Associate Professor of Surgery, University of Michigan

Dr. Brown is a plastic surgeon and physician-scientist working at the intersection of plastic surgery and regenerative gene therapy. He coined the term *genoplastic surgery* to describe the gene-level interventions his lab is building for limb loss, wound healing, and tissue regeneration.

He trained in plastic surgery and completed research training supported by the **NIH** and the **Duke Office of Physician-Scientist Development**. The lab moved to the University of Michigan in 2026.
