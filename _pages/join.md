We're building a lab that takes regeneration biology from the bench to the clinic. If that excites you, we want to hear from you.

The lab is a space of **innovation, inspiration, and inclusion**. We welcome people from all backgrounds and career stages.

---

## Open positions

### Postdoctoral Researchers — **actively recruiting**

We are hiring postdoctoral fellows to lead projects in:

- Mouse digit regeneration and blastema biology
- AAV / gene-therapy construct design for regenerative applications
- Fetal vs. adult wound-healing molecular programs
- Comparative genomics of regenerating species

**Ideal background:** PhD or MD/PhD with strong experience in molecular biology, mouse genetics, developmental biology, gene therapy, or single-cell genomics. Independent, collaborative, and curious.

**To apply:** Email Dr. Brown with (1) a brief letter describing your scientific interests and how they connect to the lab, (2) your CV, and (3) names of three references. See [contact](/contact/).

### Graduate Students

Prospective PhD students should apply through the appropriate **University of Michigan graduate program** (e.g., Cellular & Molecular Biology, BME, Molecular & Integrative Physiology). Current Michigan students are welcome to email about rotations.

### Medical Students, Residents, and Fellows

We host medical trainees at every career stage for short and long research blocks. If you are a Michigan medical student, surgical resident, or fellow interested in regenerative gene therapy, please reach out — and especially if you are interested in a **physician-scientist career path**, we'd love to talk.

### Undergraduate Researchers

We have limited capacity for undergraduates each term. Strong candidates have completed at least one molecular biology or genetics course and can commit to a multi-semester project.

---

## What you can expect

- A mentor who has lived the physician-scientist path and will help you build yours.
- Projects that lead to first-author papers and grants, not just data collection.
- A small lab where your voice shapes the science.
- Real translational ambition — we want this work in patients.

---

_Not sure if your background fits? Email anyway. The best people often don't look like the job description._
